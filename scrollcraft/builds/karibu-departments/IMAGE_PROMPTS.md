# Karibu Golf category image prompt set

Generation mode: built-in image generation tool.

Final delivery directory: `public/images/shop/categories-v2/`

## Shared style preamble

> Photorealistic premium golf editorial photography, shot on digital medium format with realistic optical depth and fine natural grain. Kenyan highland daylight, one warm brass key from camera left, cool fairway-green shadow fill, deep Coal shadows, Sand highlights and restrained red-earth warmth. Photographic realism, NOT CGI, NOT clay, NOT illustration, no plastic sheen.

Every prompt also required a portrait 3:4 category photograph with complete equipment in frame, calm negative space for HTML copy, no people, no hands, no baked-in text, no letters, no logos, no watermark and no packaging.
