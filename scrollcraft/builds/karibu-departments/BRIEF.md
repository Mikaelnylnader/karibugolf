# Karibu Golf department pages

## Direction

This is a self-authored extension of the approved Karibu Shop direction. The user explicitly delegated the creative work and asked that every department page carry the same premium scroll language and stronger category photography.

- Site: Karibu Golf department pages for Clubs, Shoes, Apparel, Bags, Balls and Accessories.
- Visitor: Kenya-based golfers who need to find the right product family quickly, then see actual available products.
- Desired outcome: choose a category, inspect the inventory and contact Karibu Golf when help is useful.
- Tone: expert, warm, practical and distinctly golf-led. Never luxury for its own sake.
- World: photographic Kenyan golf editorial. Digital medium-format realism, highland daylight, fairway green shadows, warm brass light and restrained red-earth warmth. No text or logos baked into imagery.
- Type: the existing Karibu display and body families only.
- Palette: Fairway `#1f5132`, Coal `#14110e`, Sand `#efe3cc`, Brass `#c89947`.
- Motion: Scroll Craft flow, parallax, reveal, kinetic type and restrained object tilt. No generated scrub video, no automatic sound, and no inaccessible content hidden behind motion.

## Grammar and structure

Grammar: Gallery / catalog, used as a chaptered object index.

The hero is category one, already visible and labelled. A sticky category ledger replaces the main shop's Fairway Trail. Every subsequent category is a large editorial chapter with the same factual label schema: category, available product count, short buying purpose and destination. The inventory grid is administrative and remains normal document flow.

This differs from the existing Karibu Shop fingerprint in five dimensions:

1. Nav: accumulating category ledger rather than a department progress trail.
2. Hero: the first category itself, full bleed, rather than a layered multi-department shop window.
3. Sequence: object-one hero, alternating category chapters, inventory shelf and inquiry label plate.
4. Close: a compact inquiry label plate rather than a three-part service ledger.
5. Signature: the Kit Ledger permanently stamps every visited category and resolves into a complete kit at the inventory shelf.

Shared dimension: Gallery / catalog grammar. Gate result: 5 of 6 dimensions differ from Karibu Shop, so the plan passes.

## Journey beats

1. See the first useful category immediately.
2. Understand the department's full category range.
3. Move through each category as a distinct photographic object chapter.
4. Arrive at the real available inventory.
5. Leave with a direct category path or a WhatsApp route for help.

## Feeling curve

1. Recognition: the department opens on a clear, familiar golf object and its factual label.
2. Curiosity: the next category enters from a different spatial direction and the ledger stamps it as visited.
3. Confidence: the middle categories establish breadth without losing navigation.
4. Delight: the final category completes the Kit Ledger and visibly resolves the department route.
5. Control: the real product shelf appears with prices and stock states.
6. Readiness: the final inquiry plate offers one plain way to ask for a recommendation.

## Peak

The final category enters and the whole Kit Ledger becomes complete, turning the scroll into a record of the visitor's department route.

Silence before the peak: the penultimate category uses a calmer Sand-ground composition with less type and no extra pointer effect.

## Tell-someone test

It is the golf shop where every category you pass gets stamped into your kit, and the full product shelf arrives when the kit is complete.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset and depth | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far ground | Fairway / Coal colour field | Static or smallest parallax | Never competes with text |
| Category photograph | Coherent campaign still | Restrained parallax or reveal | Entire product family remains legible |
| Edge crop | Image continuation or tonal block | Stronger movement on selected chapters | Frames rather than hides product |
| Category label | Semantic HTML | Stable while imagery moves | Factual, concise, high contrast |
| Kit Ledger | Fixed semantic navigation | Accumulates visited states | Fully keyboard operable and collapses on phones |

Opening: category one is already fully readable and visually complete.

Midpoint: alternating chapters vary image scale, direction and ground while the Kit Ledger accumulates a durable trace.

Resolved exit: the last category completes the ledger, then the live inventory appears without a pinned delay.

## Mobile and reduced motion

- Mobile uses a bottom category ledger with horizontal access to every category and no obscured content.
- Images are recropped independently using `object-position`; headings step down one type rung.
- Reduced motion keeps all images, labels and links in flow, removes positional animation and preserves the ledger's visited state.
- No essential action depends on hover, pointer or animation.

## Verification contract

- Desktop: opening, every category midpoint, inventory arrival and final inquiry plate.
- Phone: 390 by 844 and compact 360 by 640.
- Reduced motion: categories remain reachable and ledger navigation remains usable.
- Functional: category links, product links, WhatsApp CTA, console, failed requests, broken images, focus order and horizontal overflow.
- Visual: one coherent photo grade, no duplicated assets inside a department, no hidden copy, and a clearly dominant final-category peak.
