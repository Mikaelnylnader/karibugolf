# Verification: Karibu Golf P·790 product dossier

## Grammar decision

The shipped grammar is **Product dossier**. It keeps the purchase interface first, then changes form as the visitor moves from product geometry to construction, gapping and finally ordering.

The eight stock grammars lost for concrete reasons:

- Filmic one-shot would delay price, stock and configuration behind a narrative opening.
- Chaptered editorial is too text-led for a page whose strongest evidence is product geometry and exact specifications.
- Live surface implies a real-time tool or demo; the configurator records preferences rather than simulating the club.
- Continuous world would invent a spatial journey that the supplied studio photographs do not contain.
- Typographic poster would make the words dominate the iron.
- Gallery / catalog is already used by the Shop and Department builds and is designed for browsing ranges, not understanding one product.
- Split stage would force every type of evidence into one repeated two-pane composition.
- Rhythmic cutlist is too fast for comparison of technology and a seven-club specification set.

## Signature move and fingerprint result

Signature move: the exact 4–PW lofts progressively reveal into one seven-club gapping spine beside the iron face. It uses the product's real 20°, 23°, 26.5°, 30°, 34°, 39° and 44° specifications.

- Versus Karibu Shop: 6/6 dimensions differ.
- Versus Karibu Departments: 6/6 dimensions differ.

## Journey and feeling check

| Beat | Intended feeling | Device | Felt in the cold contact-sheet pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase | Clarity | Flow | Immediate and useful; gallery, price, stock and options remain the first task |
| Product study | Focus | Pin + parallax + directional reveal | The supplied views assemble as a technical portrait without hiding the product |
| Technology | Understanding | Horizontal pan | Reads as one connected construction system, with enough time per verified detail |
| Detail silence | Calm | Flow + image reveal | The beige two-column spread creates the intended pause before the peak |
| Loft trace | Confidence | Pin + progressive reveal | Clearly dominant; all seven values resolve and the longest span feels earned |
| Reference | Control | Flow table | Returns the animated story to exact, copyable specifications and source |
| Close | Readiness | Flow | Ends on the configuration decision, then offers related products |

Intended and felt curves matched after two fixes. The first contact-sheet pass showed low-opacity copy producing phone and reduced-motion contrast failures, so text fades were removed and motion was left on product imagery and the loft trace. The keyboard pass then found focused links inside flow-reveal containers, so reveal animation was removed from every container that owns an interactive control.
