# Karibu Golf P·790 product dossier

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Direction

- Site: the TaylorMade P·790 product page inside the Karibu Golf catalogue standard.
- Visitor: a Kenya-based golfer comparing a premium iron set who still needs clear local stock, pricing and ordering support.
- Desired outcome: understand the club, inspect the supplied photographs and specifications, choose a configuration and continue to WhatsApp.
- Tone: technically assured, calm and useful. The product is the authority; motion never substitutes for facts.
- World: a dark workshop dossier moving into warm Karibu sand, with the existing Fairway, Coal, Sand and Brass palette. User-supplied product photography stays accurate and unaltered.
- Type: the existing Karibu display and body families only.
- Motion: one composed sequence built from flow entry, a pinned multi-plane product study, a horizontal technology rail, progressive specification reveals and a settled close.
- Non-negotiables: gallery zoom, live catalogue price and stock, selectable configuration, WhatsApp handoff, source link, related products, semantic markup, keyboard access, mobile layout and reduced-motion parity.

## Grammar

Grammar: Product dossier.

The purchase interface is the opening fact sheet and remains normal document flow. The scroll narrative begins only after a visitor can already see the name, price, stock, gallery and options. A pinned image study then establishes shape and construction, a horizontal rail explains the four verified technologies, and a real 4–PW loft trace resolves the specifications before the page returns to configuration and related products.

Constraints for this grammar:

- Commerce and stock appear before any long-form motion.
- Every motion chapter must explain a supplied image, verified feature or real specification.
- The dominant visual object is the iron, not a lifestyle scene.
- No fake performance numbers, autoplay video, spotlight, magnet, scroll cue or full-screen marketing slogan.
- The close returns control to ordering rather than ending on a brand statement.

## Journey beats

1. Inspect the product and local buying facts without waiting for animation.
2. See the cavity, face and address views assemble into one technical portrait.
3. Move across the four verified technology notes as a connected construction story.
4. Watch the exact 4–PW loft progression become the product's gapping spine.
5. Reach the full reference table and manufacturer source.
6. Return to the buying decision or continue to related irons.

## Feeling curve

1. Clarity: price, stock, photos and options are immediately available.
2. Focus: the surrounding page falls quiet and the iron fills the visual field.
3. Understanding: construction details arrive one at a time with the relevant images.
4. Confidence: the exact 4–PW specifications are revealed as a readable progression.
5. Control: all facts settle into a normal table, source and ordering route.

## Peak

The seven-club loft trace completes from 4-iron at 20° to pitching wedge at 44° while the face view holds beside it, making the set's gapping visible as one resolved instrument.

Authored silence before the peak: the detail chapter preceding the specification trace uses a restrained two-column layout, one still image and no kinetic headline.

## Tell-someone test

It is the iron page where the actual 4–PW lofts build themselves into the spine of the set.

## Mobile and reduced motion

- Phone layout replaces overlapping image planes with a scrollable, stacked technical study and a native horizontal technology rail.
- The loft trace becomes a complete vertical list; no club value is hidden or truncated.
- Reduced motion shows every image, feature and specification in settled document flow and keeps all commerce controls operable.
- No essential action depends on hover, pointer movement or animation.

## Verification contract

- Desktop: opening commerce, pinned study midpoint, technology rail endpoint, loft trace peak, source and close.
- Phone: 390 by 844 plus compact 360 by 640.
- Reduced motion: all photographs, technology copy and seven loft values visible without positional animation.
- Functional: gallery, zoom dialog, configuration selections, WhatsApp link, section navigation, related product links, source link and keyboard focus.
- Technical: no console errors, failed images, inaccessible hidden content or horizontal page overflow.
- Visual: one dominant peak, readable product geometry, no repeated card wall and no motion without explanatory value.
