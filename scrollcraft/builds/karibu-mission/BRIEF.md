Content and placement refinement under the owner's explicit instruction to improve the supplied suggestion. Existing brand/art direction is retained, not re-interviewed. The owner confirmed that junior support, trade-ins and equipment donation programmes are **future plans**.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

**Public commitment ledger.** The unit is a stated ambition with its current status, intended action and partner invitation. Local text anchors navigate four areas. It forbids invented impact totals, applications for programmes not launched, donation checkout, testimonials, numbered cinematic chapters and opaque programme availability. Short natural flow, a held ledger heading and no forced scroll spans.

Compared with Shop, Departments, P790, Stock and About, the grammar, navigation, hero, sequence, close and signature differ (6/6). Journal shares readable editorial pacing and a quiet close, but differs in grammar, local status navigation, the offset photographic opening, commitment-led sequence and growth-path signature (5/6). The Journal's printed split-spine cover is not repeated. New page, not a replacement for any existing page.

## Journey / feeling curve, written before implementation

1. Clarity: title and concise mission beside a separate photographic window.
2. Trust: plain distinction between what Karibu does today and the programmes being planned.
3. Possibility: four factual ambition entries, reached through useful anchors; heading holds while prose stays readable.
4. Optimism (peak): more access, more players and more opportunities connect into a single drawn growth path. Not a forecast or a quantitative impact claim.
5. Agency: partner options lead to a real WhatsApp inquiry, with no false application/donation mechanism.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the mission page where more access, more players and more opportunities connect into one future for East African golf." No empty or authored-silence scroll. The peak has the largest visual change, not extra filler distance. The ending retains an actionable partner desk.

## Devices / layer contract

- Opening: kinetic semantic headline, clipped photographic window and independent slow photo/near badge planes. Copy remains on paper; nothing moves across it. Mobile stacks the picture below title and primary action.
- Current position: flow + restrained entrance. No administrative pinning.
- Four ambitions: native sticky ledger title with separately revealed entries and local anchor navigation. On phones, natural flow only.
- Peak: bespoke scroll-drawn SVG path and arriving brand-scale markers beside real text, driven from painted progress. Shared engine unchanged.
- Close: normal-flow collaboration desk with visible focus, clickable questions and useful destination.

## Content boundaries / placement

- Homepage: short mission section after "Good Golf. Real People." and before contact. Preserve "Your Next Find", stock and shop sections.
- About: improve the existing origin copy and add a mission link. Keep founder and customer-clinic sections intact.
- Dedicated route: `/growing-the-game/`, exported into `dist/static` and sitemap, linked in the footer.
- Planned programmes use "we aim", "we want", "future" and "not yet launched". Scholarships, sponsorships, trade-ins, donations and school partnerships are not sold as operating services. No verified sustainability certifications are available; use intentions about durability, reuse and less waste, not green product claims.
