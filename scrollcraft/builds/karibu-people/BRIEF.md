# Karibu People section

Targeted extension to the established Karibu Golf landing page. Existing brand, audience, page order and assets are retained; this is not a new-page redesign.

## Reused brief decisions

1. **Vibe:** Premium, welcoming, Kenyan, editorial and human.
2. **Journey:** The live-stock scene resolves, the visitor meets the people behind the shop, the three support promises become clear, then the page closes with WhatsApp contact.
3. **Energy:** Quiet entry, confident headline, warm supporting detail, calm resolution.
4. **Feeling and peak:** Recognition becomes trust. The remembered moment is “Good Golf” giving way to “Real People” as the Karibu mark settles into place.
5. **Distinctive move:** A golf-pin orbit and a growing central seam connect the human promise to the support accordion.
6. **Aesthetic range:** Premium-minimal with a restrained editorial reveal.
7. **World:** A distinct light chapter inside the established landing-page sequence, not a new continuous world.
8. **Assets:** Existing Karibu badge, typography, colours, copy and accordion.

## Purpose

- Audience: Golfers and golf-curious customers across Kenya.
- Belief: Karibu is a real Nairobi-based team that will personally help with equipment and delivery.
- Next action: Continue to “Let’s Talk Golf” and contact Karibu on WhatsApp.
- Art direction: Karibu’s existing forest, paper, lime and brass visual system.

## Feeling curve

1. **Notice:** The section label and orbit arrive softly.
2. **Confidence:** “Good Golf” rises into place.
3. **Warmth (peak):** “Real People” follows and the badge settles.
4. **Trust:** The divider completes while the three practical support promises arrive in sequence.

Peak: “The words Real People land as the Karibu badge settles beside them.”

Tell-someone sentence: “It’s the section where the brand promise unfolds into the real help Karibu provides.”

Authored silence: The first fraction of the entry is intentionally quiet so the two headline lines can land separately.

## Device score

| Beat | Device | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Notice | Flow entrance | Keeps the transition out of the stock scene calm. |
| Confidence | Clipped line reveal | Makes the headline readable while giving it a clear scroll response. |
| Warmth | Rotational badge settle | Adds brand character without moving body copy. |
| Trust | Growing seam and stagger | Connects the promise to the accordion in reading order. |

This is a targeted section enhancement, so the full-page fingerprint gate and registry row are not applicable.
