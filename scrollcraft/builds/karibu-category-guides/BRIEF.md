# Karibu category guide system

## Basis

This is an in-place expansion of the existing Karibu Golf category routes. The owner asked for the putter page's richer scroll-led education to continue across drivers, irons, wedges, golf bags and the rest of the shop. Existing Karibu photography, navigation, live catalogue and pricing remain the source of truth. No new imagery is generated.

## Visitor and outcome

The visitor knows the category they are shopping but may not know which design choices matter. Each page should teach one useful decision model, let the visitor choose a starting point, then hand them into the live products without turning the catalogue into a long article.

## Authored decisions

- Vibe: East African golf editorial, practical, confident, tactile.
- Journey: place and category, three decisions, one category-specific fit line, live collection, direct help.
- Energy: cinematic opening, calmer explanation, one interactive peak, practical resolution.
- Feeling curve: focus, orientation, curiosity, confidence, resolve.
- Memorable moment: the same fitting line changes meaning across the shop, becoming ball flight, set gapping, sole interaction, carried load or hand contact.
- Range: editorial premium rather than maximalist or software-like.
- World: distinct category scenes inside one recognizable Karibu shop system.
- Assets: existing category and product photography only.

## Page family and grammar

This is maintenance of the existing gallery/catalog grammar, not a new site fingerprint. The pages share navigation and product-shelf resolution, but their middle act changes by equipment family:

- Flight lab: drivers, fairway woods and hybrids.
- Set map: irons and wedges.
- Carry plan: golf bags and junior sets.
- Contact lab: balls, gloves and grips.
- Course kit: range finders, shoes, apparel, hats and accessories.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far environment | Existing category photograph | Slow scale and vertical travel | Remains photographic and category-specific |
| Mid field | Category-specific SVG flight, gap, turf or load line | Draws and shifts independently | Explains the decision rather than decorating it |
| Near field | Measurement marks and crop frame | Stronger bounded travel | Never covers title or controls |
| Typography | Semantic HTML | Stable | Category name, purpose and product route remain readable |

## Feeling curve

1. Focus: one category fills the frame in a real Kenyan golf setting.
2. Orientation: three practical decisions replace feature-list noise.
3. Curiosity: the fit line turns the category into a visible system.
4. Confidence: an accessible selector returns a grounded starting point.
5. Resolve: the live collection arrives with prices, stock and direct help.

## Peak and signature

Peak sentence: the fit line changed shape when I chose how I play.

Tell-someone sentence: it is the golf shop where every category gives you a different fitting instrument before it shows you products.

Signature move: **Karibu Fit Line**. One page-local instrument changes its geometry and language by category family. It is a flight window for woods, a gapping ladder for irons, a turf trace for wedges, a load map for bags, and a contact field for balls, gloves and grips. The chosen state remains visible when the product collection arrives.

## Responsive and fallback contract

- Desktop uses a held layered hero, lateral decision rail and interactive fit instrument.
- Phone recomposes the hero, turns the rail into native horizontal reading and keeps every choice at least 44px high.
- Reduced motion resolves the hero, rail and selected default in natural flow.
- No JavaScript keeps all education, default advice and the catalogue readable in source order.
