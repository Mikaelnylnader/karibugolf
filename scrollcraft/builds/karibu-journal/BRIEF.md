# Karibu Journal Scroll Craft Brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The user asked to use the same Scroll Craft quality already approved for the Karibu shop. Existing Karibu brand assets, published articles, and prior feedback are reused.

## Creative direction

- Vibe: Kenyan golf editorial, welcoming, informed, premium, grounded.
- Journey: printed journal cover, cover opens, featured guide, practical reading room, quiet colophon.
- Energy: quiet cover, rising anticipation, one decisive opening, confident browsing, calm resolution.
- Aesthetic: editorial. Paper grounds, folio labels, asymmetric spreads, hard chapter boundaries.
- World: distinct printed chapters, not one unbroken environment.
- Assets: existing Karibu photography and category imagery only. No generated assets.

## Audience, belief and action

- Audience: golfers and golf-curious readers in Kenya, especially people starting out or making practical buying and course decisions.
- Belief: Karibu Golf is a useful, locally aware guide, not only a shop.
- Primary action: Read a guide.

## Feeling curve

1. Curiosity. A restrained printed title page holds almost everything back.
2. Anticipation. The central spine begins to separate as the first photograph approaches.
3. Recognition. A Nairobi golf image opens into a useful beginner story.
4. Confidence. The complete reading room presents practical Kenya-specific answers with consistent labels.
5. Resolve. The final colophon leaves one clear next read and one human contact path.

## Peak and signature move

- Peak: “The paper cover split down its spine and opened onto a Kenyan fairway.”
- Signature move: a page-local split-spine reveal. Two paper leaves part in opposite directions while the feature photograph and story become visible beneath them.
- Tell-someone sentence: It’s the golf journal where the printed cover cracks open onto a Kenyan fairway.

## Authored silence

The opening title page is intentionally quiet. It contains no image above the fold and no scroll cue. This is anticipation, not an unloaded state.

## Grammar and score

Grammar: Chaptered editorial. No continuous drift, scrub video, media beneath type, magnetic CTA, or pinned crossfade copy.

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | Flow + in-view type | A printed cover should arrive as a complete page. |
| Opening | Bespoke split-spine + reveal | The single transformation moves the reader from cover to lived golf. |
| Feature | Parallax media column | Adds depth while keeping photograph and text in separate columns. |
| Reading room | Flow + stagger + restrained tilt | Supports scanning real articles without an identical card grid. |
| Colophon | Reveal + stable close | Resolves as a masthead plate with links that hold. |

## Fingerprint gate

- Versus Karibu Shop: differs on grammar, navigation, hero, act sequence, close, and signature move (6/6).
- Versus Karibu Departments: differs on all six dimensions (6/6).
- Versus Karibu P790 Product: differs on all six dimensions (6/6).

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper ground | Sand editorial canvas | Static | Maintains title-page legibility. |
| Rear photograph | Existing golf-moment image | Slow vertical parallax | Never crosses under story text. |
| Paper leaves | Two solid paper panels | Opposing horizontal transforms | Meet cleanly at the spine when closed. |
| Spine rule | Brass vertical line | Scales and fades | Marks the physical hinge. |
| Typography | Semantic HTML | Stable or in-view rise | Always readable and selectable. |

## Verification targets

Desktop 1440 × 1000, phone 390 × 844, compact phone 360 × 640, and reduced motion. Verify opening, split midpoint, resolved feature, reading room, colophon, all article links, keyboard focus, images, console, and overflow.
