# Karibu Putters category

## Basis

This is an in-place expansion of the existing Karibu Golf Clubs / Putters catalogue. The owner supplied the live route and explicitly requested Scroll Craft treatment, more putter education, and a clear account of the styles most used on Tour. Creative decisions reuse the approved Karibu Golf visual language, existing product photography, live catalogue and existing navigation rather than inventing a new brand system.

## Visitor and outcome

The visitor is a golfer comparing putters who may know product names but not the practical difference between blade, wider blade, mallet and zero-torque forms. The page should help them understand the tradeoffs, identify a sensible starting point for their stroke and alignment needs, then enter the live product collection.

## Truth boundary

- Tour statistics are labelled as 2025 PGA TOUR season results, not a universal or permanent market share claim.
- PGA TOUR reported 35 of 47 winners used mallets and 12 used blades in 2025.
- Shape alone does not determine fit. Neck, shaft axis, toe flow, length, lie, loft, aim and feel matter.
- The page recommends fitting and practice testing. It does not claim that the most common Tour choice is automatically right for every golfer.

## World

Technical putting studio meets Kenyan evening green. Off-black, fairway green, warm paper and restrained brass stay inside the existing Karibu system. Existing product photography is used as the object evidence. Fine contour lines and face-path diagrams provide the technical layer.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset and depth | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far environment | Existing Kenya putting-green category photograph | Slow scale and vertical drift | Holds the sense of place and never competes with copy |
| Midground | SVG green contour and target line | Small independent drift | Describes aim, not decoration |
| Focal subject | Scotty Cameron blade product photograph | Short restrained travel | Remains fully readable as a putter and never duplicates the background subject |
| Near foreground | Ball / cup aperture and measurement ticks | Stronger bounded drift | Frames the focal object without covering the title |
| Typography | Semantic HTML at the lead edge | Stable | Complete title, purpose and route to products remain readable |

Opening: a putter head sits over a darkened Kenyan green with a visible start line.

Midpoint: the object moves away while the line becomes a fitting trace, handing the visitor into shape comparison.

Resolved exit: the start line lands on the next section's three-shape index.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Intended feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focus | One putter, one line and one target establish the task |
| 2 | Recognition | Blade, wide blade and mallet become a legible spectrum rather than jargon |
| 3 | Curiosity | A quiet Tour question interrupts the catalogue rhythm |
| 4 | Surprise | The 35-to-12 winner split assembles as a physical bag balance |
| 5 | Confidence | The visitor can choose a stroke profile and see a grounded starting point |
| 6 | Resolve | The live Karibu collection arrives with prices, availability and direct product routes |

Adjacent acts intentionally change emotion. The product grid is the useful resolution, not a second spectacle.

## Peak and memory hook

Peak sentence: the Tour bag tipped toward mallets and every winning marker settled into place.

Tell-someone test: it is the putter page where 47 PGA TOUR wins physically sort themselves into mallet and blade bags as you scroll.

Signature move: `Tour Bag Balance`, a page-local 47-marker field driven by the peak act's scroll progress. Thirty-five round markers settle on the mallet side and twelve blade-shaped markers settle on the blade side, with the source and season always visible. It is not a generic Scroll Craft device and does not modify the shared engine.

## Journey and devices

1. Layered pinned object hero: pin + parallax + kinetic line, 2.2vh.
2. Shape spectrum: pan with four editorial stops, 3.5vh.
3. Quiet question: short flow + in, approximately 0.7vh.
4. Tour Bag Balance peak: pin + bespoke marker choreography, 3.1vh.
5. Fit starting point: interactive flow + SVG trace and accessible buttons, approximately 1.2vh.
6. Live product shelf and inquiry: natural flow + stagger.

The four device families are pin, parallax, pan and flow/reveal. No family repeats consecutively. The experience before the catalogue is targeted at roughly 10.7 viewport-heights on desktop; the product shelf remains natural flow because it is actual inventory, not pacing filler.

## Fingerprint status

This is a maintenance expansion of the existing Karibu Departments / category page, not a new standalone site. It deliberately preserves the category route, catalogue grammar, Kit Ledger and collection close. The new signature peak and educational acts are documented as a maintenance note after validation; no new fingerprint row is claimed.

## Responsive and fallback contract

- Desktop keeps the hero depth, horizontal shape travel and held Tour balance.
- Phones recompose the hero vertically, keep all putter heads whole and turn the shape rail into a native horizontal scroll region when motion is reduced.
- Reduced motion keeps the complete hero poster, all Tour figures and every product accessible without positional movement.
- No JavaScript keeps the source order, all copy, the selected-default fitting advice and the product catalogue usable.
