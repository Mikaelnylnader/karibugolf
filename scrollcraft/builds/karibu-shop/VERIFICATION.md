# Karibu Shop Verification

## Result

Shipped to `https://karibugolf.com/shop/` after a clean Netlify preview check.

The brief was self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The existing Karibu brand guide, live catalog structure and user-requested category hierarchy supplied the factual constraints.

## Creative system

- **Grammar:** Gallery / catalog. The shop needs fast object discovery and direct category access, so the other seven grammars lost to utility.
- **Signature move:** The Karibu Fairway Trail. It grows with page progress, marks the nearest department and remains clickable.
- **Fingerprint gate:** Passed. This was the first registry row.
- **World:** Photoreal Kenyan golf editorial. Fairway green, Sand, Coal and Brass with natural course light.
- **Generated assets:** Six category portraits for Clubs, Shoes, Apparel, Bags, Balls and Accessories. Built-in image generation was used, then the final PNGs were inspected together, resized and encoded as WebP.

The intended and felt curves matched after the static category grid was replaced by larger photographic scenes, alternating reveals and the persistent trail. The category gallery remained the largest visual change and the peak. The service ledger and completed trail hold the ending instead of fading into an empty footer.

## Evidence

- Production build completed and exported all shop, department, category and 153 product routes.
- Scroll Craft harness: desktop 1440×900, phone 390×844, compact phone 360×640 and reduced motion.
- Desktop length: 5.1 viewport-heights. Phone length: 8.0 viewport-heights. Compact phone: 9.2 viewport-heights.
- No dead scroll reported in any harness run.
- Browser verification confirmed all six generated images load, all six department routes return 200, the active trail reaches Accessories, trail progress reaches 1, horizontal overflow is zero, and reduced-motion reveals remain visible.
- No browser console errors or failed requests were reported.
- Visual contact sheets were inspected for the opening, intermediate reveal states, department cards, service close and mobile composition.

## Remaining real-device limit

Headless Chrome cannot prove Safari and touch behavior on a physical iPhone. This build does not use scrub video, so the primary iOS decoder risk does not apply, but a real-phone spot check is still the final device-level confirmation.
