# Karibu Shop Scroll Brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation from the request to apply Scroll Craft to the Karibu Golf shop and create better category photography.

## Creative brief

1. **Vibe:** Kenyan, premium, welcoming, confident, useful. Existing Karibu brand references: a fairway at dusk, brass-gold light, modern golf editorial photography.
2. **Journey:** Enter a contemporary Kenyan golf shop, understand the six departments immediately, browse the full range, understand the human service behind it, then choose a department or ask for help.
3. **Energy curve:** Calm arrival, rising curiosity, energetic category discovery, reassuring service, decisive close.
4. **Feeling and remembered moment:** The visitor should feel welcomed, then intrigued, then spoiled for choice, then confident. The remembered moment is the six category portraits arriving like a curated equipment wall while a fairway line records the path through the store.
5. **Unique behavior:** A persistent, clickable fairway trail grows as the visitor moves through the shop and marks every department they have passed.
6. **Aesthetic range:** Premium-minimal with editorial density. It must still behave like a useful shop, not a film that blocks browsing.
7. **Structure:** Distinct photographic scenes inside a gallery/catalog grammar, not one continuous world.
8. **Existing assets:** Karibu badge and lockup, brand guidelines, Fairway `#1F5132`, Brass `#C89947`, Coal `#14110E`, Sand `#EFE3CC`, product catalog, product images and local backend.

## Visitor belief

Karibu Golf is the easiest welcoming place to discover and source golf gear in Kenya.

## Primary action

Browse a department.

## Journey beats

1. **Arrival:** A layered shop window makes the range feel tangible.
2. **Orientation:** Six departments are immediately named and reachable.
3. **Discovery:** Each department gets an original photographic portrait and a factual category label.
4. **Reassurance:** Personal help, stock confirmation and Kenya delivery are explained plainly.
5. **Commitment:** The visitor chooses a department or messages the Karibu team.

## Feeling curve

1. **Welcome:** layered Fairway, Sand and equipment planes open the shop.
2. **Curiosity:** the department index makes the breadth legible at a glance.
3. **Delight:** six coherent photographs reveal distinct worlds without breaking the brand grade.
4. **Confidence:** real service information and backend-fed product counts replace generic promises.
5. **Resolve:** the fairway trail completes at a clear browse-or-message choice.

## Peak

“The category wall came alive and the fairway line showed me exactly where I was in the shop.” The peak lives in the department gallery and receives the strongest imagery and longest visual rhythm.

## Tell-someone sentence

It is the golf shop where a fairway trail grows through a curated wall of gear and each marker opens a real department.

## Authored silence

The short Sand-colored transition immediately before the department gallery is intentionally quieter so the photography has room to land.

## Grammar and score

**Grammar:** Gallery / catalog. It fits because the visitor's central question is what is available and how it is organised. Filmic one-shot loses to direct browsing, chaptered editorial is too text-led, live surface is not the product, continuous world makes category jumping harder, typographic poster underuses the equipment, split stage implies a false two-way comparison, and rhythmic cutlist is too hurried for considered shopping.

**Signature move:** The Karibu Fairway Trail, a page-local progress path with six clickable department markers and a completed arrival state.

**Fingerprint gate:** The registry is empty, so this first build clears every existing row by default.

| Beat | Device family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | Flow + layered parallax | Establishes a physical shop window without delaying access |
| Orientation | In-view stagger | Makes the six choices legible together |
| Discovery | Alternating reveal + restrained tilt | Lets every category image arrive as an object in a collection |
| Reassurance | Flow + in-view typography | Keeps service information quick and readable |
| Commitment | Bespoke Fairway Trail resolution | Turns the end into a useful navigation choice rather than a decorative footer |

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset and depth | Independent movement | Contact / occlusion rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far environment | Fairway-green field and shop shadow | Slowest vertical drift | Never reduces headline contrast |
| Midground | Category photograph panels | Moderate alternating drift | Panels remain fully clickable |
| Focal subject | Clubs category panel | Small scale and vertical shift | Dominant but does not cover the primary action |
| Near foreground | Sand edge and brass rule | Fastest restrained shift | Frames the scene without covering text |
| Typography | Semantic HTML | Stable | Headline and browse action remain readable at all widths |
