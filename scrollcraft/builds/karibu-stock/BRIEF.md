# Karibu Golf live stock room

Self-authored adaptation of the approved Karibu Golf site. The user asked for a
clear, attractive way to see what is in stock in the shop, a dedicated stock
page, and a smaller scroll-led version on the landing page. The existing Karibu
brand, real catalogue data and product photography are the source material.

## Direction

- Site: the live Karibu Golf inventory collection at `/shop/stock/`, plus
  supporting sections on the main shop and landing page.
- Visitor: a Kenya-based golfer who wants to know what can be bought now before
  spending time browsing the wider catalogue.
- Desired outcome: identify an available set, inspect its price and exact stock,
  then open the product page or ask Karibu on WhatsApp.
- Tone: factual, immediate and premium. Availability is the story.
- World: a Kenyan golf stock room photographed on clean paper and deep Fairway
  grounds. Real product images stay accurate and unaltered.
- Palette: Fairway `#1f5132`, Coal `#14110e`, Sand `#efe3cc`, Brass `#c89947`.
- Type: the existing Karibu display and body families only.
- Motion: normal flow, restrained image parallax, directional reveal and one
  page-local live stock ticket. No video, magnetic CTA, spotlight or kinetic
  headline.

## Grammar

Grammar: live surface.

The page is an operational view of current inventory, not a campaign or a
generic product grid. A live manifest opens the page. Each available product is
then treated as a full exhibit while one persistent stock ticket updates its
name, SKU, exact Kenya price, quantity and progress. The close hands the visitor
to the product detail or a human stock confirmation.

## Journey beats

1. See the exact number of products currently available in Kenya.
2. Read the complete live manifest without waiting for motion.
3. Move through one large product exhibit at a time.
4. Watch the stock ticket stamp the active SKU and exact price.
5. Open a product page or ask Karibu to confirm the set.

## Feeling curve

1. Recognition: the opening states the real available count.
2. Curiosity: the manifest names every club on the page.
3. Focus: individual product portraits replace the catalogue overview.
4. Confidence: the stock ticket keeps price, SKU and quantity visible.
5. Desire: the strongest product portrait and ticket alignment form the peak.
6. Control: the close gives one route to the product and one route to Karibu.

Adjacent beats intentionally change emotional register. The quiet manifest is
the authored silence before the full product exhibits.

## Peak and signature move

Peak: an active product enters the centre of the viewport and the persistent
paper stock ticket changes as one unit, stamping the exact SKU, quantity and
Kenya price beside the product portrait.

Signature move: the Karibu Stock Ticket. It behaves like a physical warehouse
ticket following the visitor through the collection and is built from real live
catalogue fields.

Tell-someone sentence: It is the golf shop where a live stock ticket follows
each club and stamps its exact Kenya price as it reaches the centre.

## Fingerprint gate

Against every existing row, the build changes at least four dimensions:

- Grammar: live surface rather than gallery/catalog, product dossier or
  chaptered editorial.
- Navigation: compact numbered stock register rather than a Fairway Trail, Kit
  Ledger, dossier tabs or journal folio.
- Hero: live inventory manifest with the real count rather than a layered shop
  window, first-category object, commerce sheet or printed cover.
- Sequence: manifest > large product exhibits > stock confirmation > human
  handoff.
- Close: availability confirmation desk rather than service ledger, collection
  label, configuration return or colophon.
- Signature: active SKU stock ticket, unique by definition.

Gate result: 6 of 6 dimensions differ from every registered Karibu build.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset and depth | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Alternating Sand, Fairway and Coal fields | Static hard cuts | Never drifts late between short acts |
| Product portrait | Real catalogue image | Restrained reveal and small parallax | Club remains recognisable and fully clickable |
| Secondary object | Product number or price crop | Stable | Supports hierarchy without covering the product |
| Stock ticket | Semantic HTML with live fields | Content update only | Always readable, keyboard-neutral and factual |
| Register | Numbered anchor navigation | Active state follows viewport | Every product remains directly reachable |

## Mobile and reduced motion

- Mobile turns the fixed register into a compact bottom strip and keeps every
  product in normal document flow.
- Product exhibits stack image before copy and step display type down one rung.
- Reduced motion removes positional movement but preserves all stock, price,
  links and product images.
- No essential information depends on hover, pointer movement or animation.

## Verification contract

- Desktop at 1440 by 1000: manifest, first exhibit, ticket update, peak, close.
- Phone at 390 by 844 and compact phone at 360 by 640.
- Reduced motion: every product and destination remains reachable.
- Functional: product count, stock filtering, live prices, product links,
  WhatsApp, images, focus order, console, failed requests and page overflow.
- Supporting pages: shop and landing page show the same filtered inventory and
  both link to `/shop/stock/`.
