The easiest way to plan a golf trip in East Africa is to choose one regional base, book tee times directly before arranging the rest of the itinerary, and leave enough flexibility for club competitions, weather and road travel. Kenya offers the strongest Nairobi-centred golf-and-safari circuit, while Uganda and Tanzania add distinctive city, highland and coastal options.

A championship course, a relaxed nine-hole layout and a wildlife-oriented resort round suit different trips. No official ranking of the "best golf courses in East Africa" settles that choice, so start with the route and access model that fit yours.

Visitor access, fees and competition calendars change. Confirm each booking with the club or a reputable golf-tour operator before paying for transport or accommodation.

## Three practical East Africa golf routes

### Route 1: Nairobi golf and a Kenya safari

Use Nairobi as the base for several rounds, then add a safari or Rift Valley stay. Magical Kenya highlights Royal Nairobi, Windsor, Karen and Muthaiga among the courses around the capital. Nairobi also gives you access to coaching, golf shops and international flight connections, making this the simplest circuit for a traveller who wants more than one round without changing hotels every night.

### Route 2: Nairobi, Kampala and Entebbe

Combine Kenya's capital with Uganda Golf Club in Kampala and Entebbe Club near the airport. The Uganda Golf Union lists a wider national network, including Palm Valley, Jinja, Mbale, Lugazi Hills, Toro and other regional clubs.

Fix the tee times before booking flights. Arrival day may not leave enough time for a round once airport formalities, city traffic and club schedules are taken into account.

### Route 3: Nairobi, northern Tanzania and Zanzibar

Play in Nairobi, then continue toward the Arusha and Kilimanjaro region before finishing at the coast. The Tanzania Golf Union lists Kilimanjaro Golf & Wildlife Estate as an 18-hole course in Usa River, Arusha Gymkhana and Moshi as nine-hole options, and Sea Cliff in Zanzibar as a nine-green course played in an 18-hole format.

This route combines golf with the landscapes travellers already associate with northern Tanzania and Zanzibar, but the distances make advance planning essential.

## Golf in Kenya

### Nairobi

Nairobi works well as the starting point for a Kenya golf holiday. Magical Kenya describes six courses within roughly 20 miles of the city and names Royal Nairobi Golf Club, Windsor Golf and Country Club, Karen Country Club and Muthaiga Golf Club among the established options.

Each has a different access model and calendar. Private clubs may prioritise members and competitions at busy times. Ask whether the booking is a public visitor round, a member-introduced guest round or part of a golf-tour arrangement.

Questions to ask before confirming a Nairobi tee time:

- Are visitors accepted on the requested date?
- Is a handicap certificate required?
- What is the complete green fee in KSh?
- Is a caddie required, and how is the caddie paid?
- Are rental clubs available in the correct hand and flex?
- Which tees should a visitor use?
- What is the dress code for the course and clubhouse?
- Is there a club competition that could affect access?

For a deeper Kenya-only shortlist, read [Best Golf Courses in Kenya](/blog/best-golf-courses-kenya/).

### Rift Valley and central Kenya

A Kenya golf safari does not have to remain in the capital. Magical Kenya presents the Rift Valley and the Mount Kenya/Aberdare region as golf destinations that can be combined with scenery, wildlife and other activities.

This leg moves from an urban parkland course to a higher-altitude or resort setting. Distances that look short on a map can take longer than expected, so plan one main transfer or activity per day instead of squeezing a morning round and a long road journey into the same schedule.

### Kenya coast

The coast adds warm-weather resort golf to a Kenya itinerary. Confirm whether the course is attached to a hotel, whether non-resident visitors are accepted and whether rental equipment must be reserved.

Humidity, heat and salt air make hydration and equipment care important. Wipe clubs after the round, dry gloves before storing them and carry more water than you would for a cool Nairobi morning.

## Golf in Uganda

### Kampala

Uganda Golf Club is the central city option in Kampala. The Uganda Golf Union's directory provides club contacts and a fee-structure link, making it a better starting point than an old travel-blog price.

Contact the club directly for current visitor access, caddie arrangements, dress code and competition dates. If you are staying in central Kampala, allow extra travel time even when the course looks close to the hotel.

### Entebbe

Entebbe Club is useful for an itinerary built around Entebbe International Airport. Its official site presents an established tournament venue, while the Uganda Golf Union lists it among the country's central-region clubs.

An airport-area course can be convenient, but do not book a tight connection around an 18-hole round. Golf takes time, and security, baggage and traffic can erase a narrow margin.

### Beyond Kampala and Entebbe

The Uganda Golf Union directory also lists clubs across eastern, western and northern Uganda. Jinja can be paired with a Nile-focused trip; Toro can fit a western Uganda itinerary; Mbale can sit within an eastern circuit.

Conditions and visitor services outside the main centres may differ. Ask specifically about:

- Current course condition.
- Number of playable holes.
- Rental clubs and golf-ball availability.
- Caddies and payment method.
- Food, changing rooms and transport.
- Mobile-money or card acceptance.

Carry essential consumables if you are travelling away from a large pro shop.

## Golf in Tanzania

### Dar es Salaam

The Tanzania Golf Union lists Dar es Salaam Gymkhana Golf Club and TPDF Lugalo Golf Club as 18-hole courses in the city. Dar can serve as a business-travel golf stop or the opening leg of a wider Tanzania trip.

Ask the club which visitor times avoid competitions and the hottest part of the day. Confirm travel time from your hotel using the conditions on the day rather than a map estimate.

### Arusha and Kilimanjaro

The official TGU directory lists:

- Kilimanjaro Golf & Wildlife Estate in Usa River: 18 holes.
- Arusha Gymkhana Golf Club: 9 holes.
- Moshi Golf Club: 9 holes.
- TPC Club south of Moshi: 9 holes.

This region fits naturally into a northern Tanzania golf-and-safari itinerary. Choose one course that fits the rest of the trip instead of collecting tee times that require constant transfers.

### Zanzibar

The TGU lists Sea Cliff Golf Resort & Spa in Mangapwani with nine greens and an 18-hole playing format. In other words, an "18-hole format" does not necessarily mean 18 separate greens.

Ask about hotel transfers, club rental and tee-time conditions before arriving on the island. If golf is central to the trip, secure the round before choosing non-refundable accommodation.

## How to choose the right courses

Start with the experience you want.

### For championship history

Look at established tournament venues and ask whether the championship layout and tees are available to visitors. A famous event name does not guarantee that a visitor should play the longest tees.

### For a first golf trip

Prioritise straightforward access, rental equipment, practice facilities and a welcoming visitor process. A shorter or less famous course can create a better day than a difficult booking at a private club.

### For golf plus safari

Choose courses that reduce backtracking. Nairobi plus the Rift Valley, Nairobi plus Mount Kenya, or Arusha plus a northern Tanzania safari are more coherent than crossing borders simply to add another scorecard.

### For a family holiday

Check whether non-golfers have a spa, pool, wildlife activity, beach or city option while the round is played. Also ask whether juniors are allowed and whether a short course, range or lesson is available.

## What to pack for an East Africa golf trip

Bring the items that are difficult to replace quickly:

- Golf shoes already broken in.
- Two gloves so one can dry.
- Sunscreen, cap and reusable water bottle.
- Light rain layer.
- Enough balls, tees and preferred grips or tape.
- Rangefinder batteries or charging cable.
- Prescription medicine and a small first-aid kit.
- A printed or offline copy of bookings and club contacts.

If you travel with your own clubs, use a padded travel cover, protect the longest shafts and check the airline's current sports-baggage rules. If you plan to rent, confirm right- or left-handed clubs, set makeup, shaft flex and shoe availability.

## Booking checklist for golf courses in East Africa

Send one message containing the information the club needs:

1. Preferred date and tee-time range.
2. Number of players.
3. Handicap Indexes, if available.
4. Visitor or reciprocal-club status.
5. Right- or left-handed rental requirements.
6. Caddie request.
7. Need for transport, a lesson or practice facilities.
8. Request for the complete price and payment method.

Ask for written confirmation. Reconfirm shortly before travel if the booking was made far in advance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best golf courses in East Africa?

"Best" depends on access, course style, condition, location and the rest of the trip. Build a shortlist from official union directories, then compare current visitor terms directly with each club.

### Can tourists play golf in Kenya?

Yes, but access varies. Magical Kenya advises advance tee-time booking because private clubs may have competitions and member periods that close or limit visitor play.

### Can I rent golf clubs in East Africa?

Rental is available at some clubs and resorts, but not everywhere. Confirm the hand, shaft, set composition and price before relying on it.

### Do I need a handicap certificate?

Some courses or visitor bookings may ask for one, while others welcome beginners or casual players. Ask the specific club. If you need to establish an official index, read the [Kenya golf handicap guide](/blog/golf-handicap-kenya-whs-guide/).

### Should I book through a golf-tour operator?

An experienced operator can help coordinate private-club access, transport, caddies and safari logistics. Direct booking may work for a simple city round. Compare the complete itinerary, cancellation terms and green fee together.

## Build the trip around confirmed tee times

Choose a base, confirm the rounds, then add wildlife, coast or city experiences around them. Three well-spaced courses leave more time for golf and the places around them, without turning every day into another long transfer.
